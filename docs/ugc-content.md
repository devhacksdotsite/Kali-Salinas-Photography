## Brand direction

The site should feel feminine, warm, editorial, and personal without becoming overly pink or template-like.

Recommended foundation:

- Warm ivory background
- Soft blush or muted rose accent
- Espresso or charcoal text
- Dusty mauve secondary tones
- Elegant serif display typography
- Clean sans-serif body typography
- Soft image framing and restrained rounded corners
- Subtle transitions that keep attention on the content

Avoid bright gradients, excessive decorative effects, heavy shadows, and generic beauty-template styling.
